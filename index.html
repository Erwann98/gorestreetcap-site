Candidate brief

You are running a 100 MWh battery.

Your objective is not simply to maximise gross revenue. A strong candidate should make money without taking careless imbalance or state-of-charge risk.

  • Each round represents one 30-minute settlement period.
  • You see the current intraday price plus imperfect system information.
  • Choose a physical battery dispatch from -50 MW (charge) to +50 MW (discharge).
  • You may hedge 100%, 50% or 0% of that physical position at the visible intraday price.
  • Any unhedged physical volume settles at the unknown imbalance price revealed after submission.
  • Battery efficiency and £3/MWh throughput cost reduce economics.
  • Your final score combines P&L, market-view accuracy, risk discipline and asset management.

Important: the system signal is probabilistic. Sometimes the obvious trade will be wrong. The point is to size and hedge decisions sensibly.
